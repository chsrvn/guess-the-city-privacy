# Privacy Policy for Guess the City

Last updated: [2026-10-03]

Guess the City ("the app") is a geography puzzle game made by Sravan.
This policy explains what information the app uses.

## Information we collect
We do not collect, store or share any personal information on our servers. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics.

## Information stored on your device
The app saves your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, daily streak and sound settings) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can also use "Reset progress" in the app.

## Notifications
If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminder notifications on your device about your daily challenge and streak. These are local notifications created on your phone. You can turn them off at any time in your device's settings.

## Maps and third-party services
When a map is shown after you solve a city, the app loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) and a map library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. These services receive your device's IP address and standard request information when the map loads, as with any website. Please see their own privacy policies:
- OpenStreetMap Foundation: https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
- Cloudflare cdnjs: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
